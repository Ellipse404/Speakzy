CREAM SETUP

Control YouTube with your voice. Follow these 3 steps.

1 · Grant microphone access

Click below and allow the microphone prompt. This lets Cream hear the wake word "cream".

2 · Open YouTube

Cream runs automatically on youtube.com. You'll see a small CREAM badge in the corner when it's listening.

3 · Say a command

Prefix every command with the wake word.

  • › "cream pause" — pause the video
  • › "cream play" — resume
  • › "cream back 10 seconds" — rewind 10s
  • › "cream forward 30 seconds" — skip 30s
  • › "cream mute" / "cream unmute"
  • › "cream volume up" / "cream volume down"
  • › "cream fullscreen" — toggle fullscreen
  • › "cream next video" — go to the next video
